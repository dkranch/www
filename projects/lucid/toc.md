We ordered a Lucid Gravity and this page will go over some of the things we did. I had a [test drive](/projects/ev/gravity/) page, but this will look at the experiences after that point.

After the drive, we visited with a friend and then headed to the airport. As we talked about this on the way, we realized that we were both excited by the Gravity. It's like the next evolution of Tesla, changing and making things better. Adding some buttons, more screens, and luxury. It's still a computer on wheels, but it's a very nice case for a computer.

Ultimately, the Gravity (not Grand) isn't really much more expensive than the Volvo. It is more than the Cadillac, but it's also more exciting. I think we (surprisingly) both loved this more than the other cars. 

There were also incentives. As we were checking, we saw that they were offering $10,000 incentives on 2026s as well as 0% financing. That's a good deal, and also better than the others. They were 0.9% or 2.9%.

Ultimately, we decided to see if one would suit us, and ended up [purchasing](/projects/lucid/purchasing/) on in the SFO airport.
